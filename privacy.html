Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 20, 2026 • App: QuotaFolio (Android)

1. Local-Only Data Storage

QuotaFolio is designed as an offline-first Android application. Imported documents, reading schedules, session completion/skip logs, daily targets, and bookmark audit entries are stored locally on your device in Room SQLite (quotafolio.db) and internal app storage.

QuotaFolio does not require user accounts or upload your personal reading files to external servers.

2. Permissions & Device Features

To deliver scheduled reading pop-ups and render documents offline, the app requests only device-level capabilities such as notifications, exact alarms, overlay pop-up display, and boot/time receivers so schedules remain accurate across restarts and timezone changes.

3. Data Control & Deletion

You can permanently delete any imported file—along with its scheduled alarms, internal copy, and associated database history—using the two-step delete confirmation inside the app, or clear/uninstall the app from Android system settings.

4. Repository & Contact

Project updates, release notes, and issue tracking are hosted at github.com/ekt-builds/QuotaFolio.
